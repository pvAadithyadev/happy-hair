Empowering Technical Educators Across India

The Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE) is a national professional non-profit making society with the goal of providing quality training to technical education teachers.

Learn More View Events

About ISTE

ISTE is focused on improving technical education in India through faculty development programs, curriculum development, and educational technology initiatives.

Our Mission

To advance technical education through quality curriculum development, effective teaching, and meaningful assessment practices.

Our Network

With more than 70,000 lifetime members, ISTE has created a nationwide community of technical educators sharing best practices.

Training Programs

We conduct faculty development workshops, short-term training programs, and continuing education courses throughout the year.
